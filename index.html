OWEN COOPER

The face behind Adolescence

ABOUT OWEN

Owen Cooper is a young British actor from Warrington, England, who became internationally known for his outstanding performance in Netflix's series Adolescence. Surprisingly, Adolescence was his first professional acting job. Before becoming an actor, Owen dreamed of becoming a footballer and supported Liverpool. However, he later discovered his passion for acting and started attending drama classes. His first major role quickly turned him into one of the most talked-about young actors of his generation.

THE ROLE THAT CHANGED EVERYTHING

Jamie Miller in Adolescence

Owen plays Jamie Miller, a 13-year-old boy who is arrested on suspicion of murdering a classmate. His character is at the centre of the story, and the series explores his family, his relationships and the events surrounding the crime. Owen's performance is especially impressive because he had never worked professionally on a television set before.

How did he get the role?

Casting director Shaheen Baig discovered Owen through a demo tape submitted by his local drama school, Drama MOB. Around 500 boys were considered for the role. Owen stood out because of his natural acting, confidence and ability to improvise. He was then invited to a proper audition and a chemistry test with Stephen Graham, who plays his father in the series. Eventually, Owen got the role of Jamie Miller and made his professional acting debut.

ONE SHOT. NO CUTS.

One of the most challenging parts of Adolescence was its unique filming technique. Every episode was filmed in one continuous shot, without traditional cuts or editing between scenes. This meant that the actors had to remember long dialogues, move perfectly with the camera and maintain their emotions throughout each take. Owen revealed that he studied his lines for around four hours a day because he was determined to get everything right. This made his first acting experience incredibly demanding and unforgettable.

THE EMMY AWARDS

A historic achievement

In September 2025, Owen Cooper made history at the 77th Primetime Emmy Awards. At just 15 years old, he won the award for Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie for his role in Adolescence. He became the youngest male actor ever to win a Primetime Emmy Award. What makes this achievement even more incredible is that it was his very first professional acting role. Adolescence also won several other Emmy awards, including Outstanding Limited or Anthology Series.

A moment to remember

During his acceptance speech, Owen thanked his family, his co-stars and everyone who worked behind the scenes. His journey from drama classes to winning one of television's biggest awards is truly remarkable.
